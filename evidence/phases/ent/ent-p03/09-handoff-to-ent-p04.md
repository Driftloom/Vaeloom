## 3. Transferred Obligations & Focus Areas for ENT-P04

When commencing Phase `ENT-P04` (Project Planning and Delivery Governance), the
incoming program governance team must execute:

1. **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Decompose the 4-Wave Enterprise Delivery
   Baseline established in `04-priority-release-baseline.md` into work packages
   with clear ownership, estimates, and exit criteria across Phase `ENT-P05`
   through `ENT-P21`.
2. **Schedule & Critical Path Analysis (PERT/CPM):** Model task dependencies,
   critical path constraints, lead times for external vendor approvals, and
   buffer allocations.
3. **Capacity & Resource Allocation:** Model cross-functional engineering,
   security, SRE, and QA resource capacity against estimated sprint velocity.
4. **Delivery Governance Framework:** Establish steering committee cadences,
   stage-gate review procedures, exception management workflows, and RACI
   matrices.
5. **Risk Governance & Contingency Planning:** Formalize quantitative risk
   scores, exposure ceilings, and pre-allocated contingency budgets for
   high-impact enterprise failure modes.
