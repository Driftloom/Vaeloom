## 3. Transferred Obligations & Focus Areas for ENT-P02

When commencing Phase `ENT-P02` (Research, Domain Analysis, and Data Discovery),
the incoming research team must:

1. **Enterprise Capability Research:** Deepen domain models for multi-cohort
   student provisioning, career center coaching queues, and enterprise
   outplacement workflows.
2. **Design-Partner Evidence Program:** Structure empirical research protocols
   with partner institutions (e.g. universities, transition consultancies) to
   gather measured outcome data rather than anecdotal claims.
3. **Data Taxonomy & Ontology Mapping:** Map the formal 22-memory type schemas
   (including temporal validity, confidence scores, and provenance citations) to
   standardized HR-XML and Open Badges ontologies.
4. **Third-Party Connector Feasibility:** Conduct API spike research on
   enterprise ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo) and
   institutional Student Information Systems (SIS: Banner, Workday Student).
5. **Zero-Trust Memory Boundaries:** Validate that domain models maintain strict
   isolation between the Candidate Sovereign Vault and the Institutional Shared
   View under all domain workflows.
